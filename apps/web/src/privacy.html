1. Introduction

{{APP_NAME}} ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Android application (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide and improve the App:

  • Audio data: Voice notes you record or import are processed in real-time to generate translations. Audio is processed on-device or through third-party AI services only for the purpose of translation and is not stored on our servers.
  • Usage analytics: We may collect anonymous usage data (e.g., feature usage, crash reports) through third-party analytics tools to improve the App.
  • Device information: Basic device metadata (e.g., device model, OS version) may be collected for analytics and crash reporting purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide real-time voice translation services
  • To improve the App's performance and features
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues
  • To understand usage patterns and optimize the user experience

4. Data Storage

All your translations and voice notes are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your audio or translation data to our servers. You may delete your data at any time by clearing the App's data or using the in-app history delete feature.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • AI translation providers: Audio data may be sent to third-party AI services (e.g., speech-to-text and translation APIs) solely for the purpose of generating translations. These services are governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Analytics: We use anonymous analytics tools to understand how the App is used.
  • Error monitoring: Crash reports may be sent to error monitoring services to help us fix bugs.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Last updated" date above.
